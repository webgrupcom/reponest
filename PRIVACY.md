# Privacy Policy – RepoNest

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

RepoNest (by Webgrup) does **not** collect, store, sell or share personal data.

- The extension runs only on `https://github.com/`.
- It reads an organization's repository list from GitHub's own Repositories page, using your existing GitHub session in the browser, and shows it on screen as folders.
- No data is sent to Webgrup or to any third party. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising, and no server.
- Your folder rules are kept in Chrome's sync storage (`chrome.storage.sync`) and your on/off choice in `chrome.storage.local`. Folder open/closed state, order, hidden folders, view and sort choices and a one-minute cache of the repository list are kept in the page's local storage on `github.com`. This data never leaves your browser or your Chrome account.
